###### **Market Outlook**

1. **BNP Paribas Settles Cross-Border Payments in 80 Seconds in BIS Project Agorá**
   * **BNP Paribas**, alongside 27 other global commercial banks and 5 major central banks, successfully completed real-value wholesale cross-border transaction testing under Project Agorá [1, 101].
   * The trial settled approximately CHF 800,000 ($1 million) across 17 distinct scenarios and six G7 currencies in an average of 80 seconds, compared to the multiple days typically required by legacy correspondent networks [1].
   * The open-source programmable platform leverages atomic settlement, ensuring transactions complete in full or not at all [1].
   * Smart contracts further enable automated compliance checks and conditional payment execution to remove major bottlenecks [1].
   * [Read more](https://pro.edgex.exchange/en-US/news/article/bis-project-agora-tokenized-cross-border-payments-bc6da263) [1]

*   **The Compliance Shift to Parallelism:** For regulators, the primary friction in digital banking was consecutive sanctions and AML screenings slowing down transactions. Public blockchain rails are officially becoming the preferred distribution mechanism for tier-one asset management.
