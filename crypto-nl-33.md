###### **MACRO TRENDS: What's changing**

*   **The Federal Backstop Normalizes:** Following last week's capital control push, the G7 regulatory posture has taken an institutional turn. The OCC opening bank charters to crypto-native firms and World Liberty securing conditional federal approval signals that regulators are moving from reactive containment to absorbing digital asset rails into formal banking structures.
*   **Wholesale Settlement Interoperability Escalates:** Competitor asset managers continue to grow, but G7 central and commercial banks are actively executing multi-bank shared ledgers. Swift launching its tokenized deposit shared ledger with 17 global banks, alongside the Qivalis consortium's expansion to 37 banks, proves that the interbank tokenized payment network is standardizing at a rapid pace.
*   **The Physical Vulnerability Vector Expands:** Software risks remain high, but the primary threat surface has shifted decisively toward physical supply chain data leaks. Trezor and SafePal suffering consecutive fulfillment partner database breaches exposes a major operational weakness where offline cold storage is compromised by simple, physical-world database leaks.
