### Recommended policy statement

> Since Mythos, vulnerability management should move from a severity-led patching model to an exposure-led exploitation management model. For known exploited or critical internet-facing vulnerabilities, the organisation should target **risk reduction within 12–24 hours**. Risk reduction may include patching, isolation, access restriction, WAF/IPS rules, disabling vulnerable functionality, or other approved compensating controls. Full remediation should remain tracked separately until the underlying vulnerability is permanently resolved.
